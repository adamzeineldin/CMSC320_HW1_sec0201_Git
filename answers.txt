Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git would be used to collaborate with others on coding and other types of projects. It shows history of everything that has occurred in a project and allows you to go back to old commits or use different branches.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be applied to almost every field. It is used to see how a current field is progressing and can be used with computer science to make machine learning models that accomplish certain tasks and make predictions.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines two branches together and keeps the original commit history. Rebasing moves your commits on top of another branch.
